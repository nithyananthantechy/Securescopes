NIST Benchmark
NIST CSF 2.0

National Institute of Standards and Technology Cybersecurity Framework 2.0. Spans Govern (GV), Identify (ID), Protect (PR), Detect (DE), Respond (RS), and Recover (RC) functions.

  • Organizational Governance & Risk
  • Asset Management & Access Controls
  • Continuous Monitoring & Incident Response

ISO/IEC Standard
ISO/IEC 27001:2022

International standard for Information Security Management Systems (ISMS). Focuses on Annex A controls categorized into 4 themes: Organizational, People, Physical, and Technological.

  • Clause 4–10 ISMS Requirements
  • A.5 Organizational & A.6 People Controls
  • A.8 Technological Cybersecurity Controls
93 Controls / 4 Themes Assess In Engagements
CIS Benchmarks
CIS Controls v8

Center for Internet Security Critical Security Controls. Prioritized, prescriptive actions for cyber defense grouped into Implementation Groups (IG1 for MSMEs, IG2, IG3).

  • Control 1 & 2: Inventory of Enterprise & Software Assets
  • Control 3 & 4: Data Protection & Secure Configuration
  • Control 5 & 6: Account Management & Access Control
18 Controls / 153 Safeguards Assess In Engagements
Payment Security
PCI DSS v4.0

Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard. Technical and operational requirements designed to protect account data, cardholder data environments (CDE), and payment systems.

  • Req 1 & 2: Network Security Controls & System Hardening
  • Req 3 & 4: Protect Stored Account Data & Transmission Encryption
  • Req 7 & 8: Access Restrictions & MFA for Privileged Accounts
12 Core Requirements Assess In Engagements
Privacy Law
DPDP Act 2023

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023. Compliance requirements for Data Fiduciaries handling personal data, including consent management, security safeguards, and breach notification obligations.

  • Reasonable Security Safeguards to Prevent Personal Data Breach
  • Mandatory Reporting of Data Breaches to Board and Affected Principals
  • Data Retention & Purpose Limitation Controls
